WORKING METHOD / 02

Reliable data is not merely labeled.
It isdefined.

01

Decompose the scenario

Work backward from the business goal to identify model tasks, high-risk moments, confusing boundaries, and the questions the data must answer.

02

Make the rules explicit

Use label definitions, positive and negative examples, boundary cases, and adjudication principles to keep distributed work consistent.

03

Make quality observable

Connect annotation, sampling, review, and adjudication—and monitor error types instead of relying on a single pass-rate number.

04

Make errors reusable

Use bad cases to expose unclear rules, data gaps, and taxonomy problems, then turn one-off failures into durable operating assets.
