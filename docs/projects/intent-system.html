Built a customer-intent system for PICC’s auto-insurance AI support workflow, covering business classification, knowledge matching, clarification prompts, and human escalation for complex inquiries.

01 / Business Problem

One question can lead
to very different workflows.

Insurance questions often contain hidden intent. A renewal inquiry may include product comparison; a policy lookup may actually ask about the purchase process; a routine question may develop into a complaint or claims dispute. Misclassification affects the answer and can prevent high-risk cases from reaching a human agent.

CONFUSION

Renewal vs product inquiry

When price and coverage appear together, the system must identify the primary need and context.

PROCESS

Lookup vs purchase process

Separate existing-policy queries from questions about buying a new policy.

RISK

Routine inquiry vs dispute

Detect complaints and claims disputes that require escalation.

ANSWER

Knowledge match vs clarification

When information is missing, gather critical slots instead of forcing an answer match.

02 / Taxonomy Design

Identify not only the question,
but the next action.

I led the rule documentation and designed a multilayer taxonomy connecting core intent to execution strategy, giving each data item value for business routing, risk decisions, and answer handling.

01Core intentPolicy / purchase / renewal / product / fees
02Key informationVehicle, policy, timing, coverage, and customer need
03Risk and routingRisk level, rejection, clarification, or human handoff
04Answer sourceKnowledge-base entry and standard-response matching
03 / Production Loop

Rules are not written once.
They mature through disagreement.

The program covered requirement intake, scenario decomposition, taxonomy design, rule documentation, pilot annotation, production annotation, sampled QA, and bad-case capture. Each pilot disagreement was classified as a boundary issue, data gap, or interpretation gap and fed into the next rule version.

POSITIVE / NEGATIVE

Comparable boundaries

Pair label definitions with counterexamples that look similar but do not belong.

ADJUDICATION

Consistent high-risk decisions

Concentrated review for complaints, claims disputes, and human-handoff samples.

ROOT CAUSE

Prevent recurring errors

Added rules and similar samples for renewal mislabels and knowledge-matching failures.

STANDARD RESPONSE

Connect intent to action

Linked labels to knowledge sources, standard responses, and handling strategies.
