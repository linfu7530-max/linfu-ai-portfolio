CASE 02 / MODEL EVALUATION

AI Voice Calls
Three-Model Benchmark

Benchmarked Doubao, ERNIE Bot, and iFlytek Spark on the same voice-call samples, establishing one scoring standard to support model selection for an in-vehicle voice assistant.

My roleEvaluation Lead
Team4 Evaluators
Scope3 Models
01 / Evaluation Brief

Different strengths.
One shared yardstick.

The models differed in response style, context retention, and safety behavior. The program needed a repeatable, explainable evaluation method that compared performance on identical samples instead of relying on subjective impressions.

MODEL

Doubao

Observed overall stability across routine and difficult scenarios.

MODEL

ERNIE Bot

Focused on response usefulness, instruction understanding, and information completeness.

MODEL

iFlytek Spark

Focused on recognition and response stability in complex scenarios.

SCENARIOS

Routine + complex

Used two difficulty tiers to observe performance as constraints increased.

02 / Evaluation Framework

From understanding the request
to delivering a useful answer.

I defined the model set, scenario difficulty, scoring dimensions, and composite-score logic, then coordinated evaluators using transcribed prompts and model responses under one rubric.

01ComprehensionRecognizes true intent and critical constraints
02CorrectnessAccurate, complete, and actionable information
03ExperienceContext retention, naturalness, and concision
04Safety boundariesSensitive topics, safe refusal, and risk control
03 / Bad-Case Analysis

A low score is not the conclusion.
Its cause is the optimization entry point.

CONTEXT

Lost conversational context

Ignored earlier turns in multi-turn calls, producing repetitive or off-target answers.

CONSTRAINT

Missed negation and constraints

Misread negative wording or dropped conditions from complex instructions.

USEFULNESS

Generic responses

Produced polished language without information that actually solved the user’s problem.

SAFETY

Weak sensitive-topic handling

Applied inconsistent response strategies to risky or sensitive requests.

04 / Output

Turn subjective experience
into selection evidence.

The program produced a three-model evaluation guide, dimension-level scores, a composite ranking, and categorized optimization recommendations. Doubao was the most stable overall; ERNIE Bot showed weaknesses in response usefulness; iFlytek Spark was less stable in complex recognition and response scenarios.
