CASE 01 / COMPUTER VISION

In-Cabin DMS
Driver-State Recognition

Used in-cabin image and video data to identify fatigue, distraction, phone use, smoking, and other risky behaviors—creating a trainable and reviewable data foundation for vehicle safety alerts.

My roleData Operations / Team Lead
Team7 Annotators + 3 QA
Scale420K Images / Frames
01 / Challenge

Similar actions create
ambiguous label boundaries.

Risk behaviors in DMS footage rarely appear as clean textbook poses. A hand resting on the face can resemble a phone call; checking the instrument panel can resemble looking at a phone; drinking can resemble smoking. Low light, occlusion, and profile views amplify disagreement.

AMBIGUITY

Overlapping actions

Similar hand positions, head poses, and gaze directions create label confusion.

ENVIRONMENT

Real-world interference

Night scenes, facial occlusion, and profile views reduce sample decidability.

CONSISTENCY

Reviewer disagreement

A ten-person operation needs one shared standard or small biases scale with the dataset.

PRIORITY

Recall on high-risk behavior

Phone use, downward gaze, and closed-eye fatigue require special attention to missed positives.

02 / Strategy

Turn every disagreement
into a shared rule.

I owned annotation and QA allocation, delivery tracking, standard alignment, and issue reviews. For recurring disputes, I added negative examples and specialized dimensions so the team understood both what to label and why a sample should not receive a competing label.

01Core taxonomyNormal / fatigue / phone gaze / calling / smoking
02Pilot calibrationExpose interpretation gaps and boundary disputes early
03Layered QAAnnotation / sampling / review / adjudication
04Bad-case feedbackAdd negative examples, hard cases, and boundary rules
03 / Deliverables

Not just a dataset.
A quality operating system.

TAXONOMY

Core labels + specialist dimensions

Added gaze deviation, both hands off the wheel, occlusion, and uncertainty dimensions.

HARD-CASE RULES

Negative examples for similar actions

Defined exclusions for face-touching, checking the dashboard, drinking, and other confusing behaviors.

OPERATING MODEL

Annotation–QA–adjudication loop

Set capacity targets by image and video task type and tracked mislabels, misses, and disputed samples.

DATA VALUE

Support for real driving conditions

Improved the data foundation for low light, occlusion, profile views, and visually similar actions.
